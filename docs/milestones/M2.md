# Milestone 2: link to Shopify page

Status: done, QA passed ([qa/milestone-2.md](../qa/milestone-2.md)) and every blocking and
should-fix finding is fixed. Last commit checked: `02a426c`. At that commit `pnpm lint` reports
0 warnings, `tsc` passes, all 755 tests pass and `pnpm build` passes.

Demo: paste a link, confirm the product at Gate 1, pick a supplier and a price at Gate 2, review
and edit the AI page copy at Gate 3, then approve. The draft product on the Shopify store is
published. The whole flow runs in mock mode on a local PGlite database (see "Demo in mock mode"

## Delivered

- **SupplierFinder** (`lib/sourcing`):
  - **Live** (`live.ts`): Google Lens through SerpApi on the analysis reference image, then
    Google Shopping on the main keyword when Lens finds too few results. Listing pages are read
    with a Firecrawl JSON scrape (3 at a time). If a scrape fails, the search-result data is used
    when it has a price. Listings without a price are dropped. The rest are ranked by match
    score (5 by default, 10 at most).
  - **Mock** (`mock.ts`): deterministic listings from AliExpress, CJ Dropshipping, Alibaba and
    Temu for the sample product, with fixed demo exchange rates and the same usage metering as
    live.
  - Listings rejected at Gate 2 are passed as `excludeUrls` and never offered again.
  - Every call goes through the brand proxy or the SSRF-guarded client.
- **Margin and quote** (`lib/sourcing/margin.ts`):
  - Unit economics in integer cents: landed cost, payment and Shopify fees, gross margin,
    break-even CPA, max CPA at the target margin, and break-even ROAS.
  - `quoteSupplier` suggests the selling price and the compare-at price from the brand pricing
    rule. If that price misses the brand's minimum margin, it is raised to the lowest price
    that meets it.
  - The reviewer can override the price, the compare-at price, other costs per order and the
    exchange rate.
- **Claude copywriter** (`lib/ai/copywriter-live.ts`):
  - Default model `claude-opus-5-5` (`AI_COPYWRITING_MODEL`), with structured output validated
    by `pageCopySchema`.
  - It writes the title, description HTML, highlights, FAQ, guarantee, ad texts and SEO in the
    brand language.
  - Gate 3 rejection notes steer the next version (`CopyHint`).
- **Copy-safety guard** (`lib/ai/copy-safety.ts`):
  - `findUnsafeClaims` flags fake reviews and ratings, customer or sales counts, endorsements,
    fake popularity, superlatives, health and certification claims, absolute guarantees, false
    urgency, prices and discounts, and URLs or emails.
  - The live copywriter regenerates once with the findings. A second hit fails the call.
  - `sanitizeDescriptionHtml` is a strict allowlist: `p ul ol li h2 h3 h4 strong em b i br`,
    with no attributes.
- **Pipeline engines** (`PIPELINE_ENGINE`):
  - `workflow` is Vercel Workflow (`workflow@5.2.3`, `withWorkflow` in `next.config.ts`). It is
    durable: each run waits on a hook at each gate, and steps retry with backoff (30 s, doubling
    up to 10 min, 5 attempts).
  - `inline` runs the same steps synchronously inside the service call.
  - Both engines implement the `ProductPipeline` interface. The new steps are `source` (Gate 1
    to Gate 2) and `build` (Gate 2 to Gate 3).
- **Gate 2** (`selectSupplier`, `rejectSuppliers`, `quoteSupplierChoice`):
  - Choose one listing, the target store and the prices. A margin below the brand minimum needs
    an explicit "go on anyway".
  - The approval, selection, prices, margin and status move happen in one transaction, with a
    `gate.approve` audit entry.
  - "None of these" records the rejected URLs and runs the search again.
- **Gate 3, page part** (`approvePage`, `rejectPage`, `regeneratePageCopy`, `updatePageCopy`):
  - **Approve** publishes the page.
  - **Reject** (with a note) and **Regenerate** write new copy on the same Shopify draft.
  - **Edit** saves human changes and pushes them to the draft. Edits are sanitised and checked
    by the guard.
  - The videos part of Gate 3 is M3, so the product stays at Gate 3 after the page approval.
- **Shopify** (`lib/integrations/shopify`):
  - **Idempotent drafts**: `productSet` with the Trendcart product id as a custom ID metafield
    (`trendcart.product_id`), so a re-run updates the same product. Variants are matched by
    option values. Trendcart images are matched by a deterministic filename. Merchant media is
    never touched.
  - Every input is sanitised before it is sent (`sanitizeDraftInput`).
  - `publishProduct` sets the product ACTIVE and publishes it on the Online Store channel.
  - `unpublishProduct` sets it back to DRAFT with `productUpdate(status: DRAFT)` (2026-10;
    `productChangeStatus` is deprecated). It is idempotent.
  - `listProductTemplates` reads `templates/product*.json` of the live theme.
  - Media deletion is ready for M3.
- **Template picker and kit**:
  - `listShopifyTemplates` and `setShopifyTemplate` (Media buyer and Owner) check that the
    suffix exists in the live theme before saving it.
  - The Connections screen still shows a text field. The check runs when you save.
  - The template kit (`lib/integrations/shopify/template-kit`) adds a `product.trendcart`
    template and a section that renders `custom.highlights`, `custom.faq` and
    `custom.guarantee`. Merchant guide: [shopify-template-kit.md](../shopify-template-kit.md).
- **Product detail UI** (`/app/products/[id]`, `components/app/product/*`):
  - Gate tracker.
  - Supplier cards with a live margin preview.
  - Gate 3 page review: the description is previewed in a sandboxed iframe, with an inline
    editor, Shopify admin and preview links, a "publishing" state with auto-refresh, and a
    "Try again later" message when a rebuild limit is reached.
- **Schema**: migrations `0001` to `0003` (see "Upgrade notes"). This closes M1 open questions
  1 to 3.

## QA fix round

| Finding | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **B1** publish before the gate re-check | `approvePage` is now **claim, then publish, then record**. (1) Under the product lock, the claim re-checks the status, the open page part and the **copy version**. It then records the approval with `state: "publishing"`, and from that point every other page action is refused. (2) Outside the transaction, the draft is synced and published. (3) A second transaction sets the approval to `published` and the `shopify_product` row to active, and writes the `gate.approve` and `page.publish` audit entries. If step 2 or 3 fails, the product is set back to DRAFT on Shopify, the approval is voided (kept as history) so the review reopens, `page.publish_revert` is audited with error codes only, and the error is rethrown. A claim older than **5 minutes** (`STALE_PUBLISH_CLAIM_MS`) is voided as `stale_claim` and taken over by the next approval |
| B1 copy version | `pageCopyVersion` (`lib/workflows/copy-version.ts`) is the first 16 hex characters of a sha256 over canonical JSON of the stored copy. The UI sends the version it rendered. A mismatch gives "The page changed since you loaded it." and the dialog offers **Reload**. Any edit or regeneration changes the version |
| S1 human edits not sanitised | `updatePageCopy` sanitises the description. `sanitizeDraftInput` also runs on every path to Shopify and turns every other field into plain text |
| S2 unterminated tag survived the sanitizer | The sanitizer is rewritten as a tokenising allowlist. Every `<` that does not start a complete allowed tag is escaped |
| S3 guard only in the live copywriter | The guard also runs in the build step (unsafe copy fails the step, retryable, and is never stored or pushed) and in `updatePageCopy` (refused, see "Decisions") |
| S4 phrasings got past the guard | New patterns: star glyphs, spelled-out and "k/M" counts with audience or sales nouns, "trusted by" and "recommended by", expert endorsements, `% off` and other discounts, prices with a currency, URLs, emails and bare domains. Patterns cover English, French, German and Spanish |
| S5 unlimited paid rebuilds | `enforceRebuildLimit`: **10 per product and 60 per workspace per sliding hour** for "none of these", page reject and regenerate. The check runs after the gate check, so a refused action does not use the quota |
| S6 rejected listings came back | `SupplierFinder.find` gets `excludeUrls` from the Gate 2 rejections, in live and mock |
| S7 expiring stores blocked Gate 2 | Gate 2 accepts stores that are `active` or `expiring` |
| N2 `javascript:` and `data:` URLs accepted on listings | `supplierListingSchema` URLs and images only accept `http:` and `https:` |
| N5 random order between decisions in the same microsecond | Each gate decision gets a monotonic per-product `payload.seq`, and `created_at` never goes before the previous decision |
| N6 scraped variant names unchecked | `safeVariantGroups` drops option names or values that the guard flags. All variant text is sent as plain text |
| N7 iframe sandbox | A comment in `page-review.tsx` says `allow-scripts` must never be added next to `allow-same-origin` |

N1, N3 and N4 are carried to M3 (below).

## Decisions

- **Unsafe claims in human edits are refused, with no override.** This is a service rule. An
  edit that trips the guard (including prices and discounts in the copy) fails with
  `invalid_input`, and the message names the field and the rule. Prices belong in the Shopify
  price fields, not in the copy.
- The approval is bound to a **content hash**, not a timestamp, so no schema change was needed.
- A failed publish is **reverted and audited**, not retried in the background. The reviewer
  approves again once the cause is fixed.
- After a successful publish, Trendcart does not unpublish. To take a page down, the merchant
  sets it back to draft in the Shopify admin. The approval dialog says so.
- Trendcart never writes themes. The merchant installs the template kit once per theme.

## Demo in mock mode

1. Set up as in [setup.md](../setup.md) (`INTEGRATIONS_MODE=mock`,
   `DATABASE_URL=pglite:./.data/dev`), then run `pnpm db:seed` and `pnpm dev`.
2. Sign in as `owner@northwind.test` (password `trendcart-demo-2026`).
3. **Connections**: connect a Shopify store. The mock store is `trendcart-demo.myshopify.com`.
   Optionally, set the product page template to `trendcart` (the mock theme has it).
4. **Import**: paste any TikTok video link. Wait for **Confirm product**, then confirm
   (Gate 1).
5. The product moves to **Finding suppliers**, then **Choose supplier**. Pick a listing and
   look at the margin preview. Try a manual price, then click **Choose this supplier**
   (Gate 2). Optionally, try **None of these** first: the next search offers other listings.
6. The page is written and created as a **draft** on the mock store. At Gate 3, open
   **Edit copy**, change a highlight and save. Then try saving "Rated 4.9 out of 5": it is
   refused.
7. Click **Approve and publish**. The page shows "Publishing to Shopify…" and then
   **Published**. The product stays at Gate 3, waiting for videos (M3).

In mock mode the "Preview the draft", "View on store" and "Open in Shopify admin" links point at
the fake `trendcart-demo` store, so they do not open a real page.

The same flow is covered end to end by `tests/integration/m2-demo-flow.test.ts`.

    relies on Vercel Queue authentication. The generated route

    the logs. Consider a 404 rewrite while no webhook is used.

13. **No browser check of the Gate 3 screen or the template picker yet.** They are covered by
    service and server-action tests only (vitest runs `*.test.ts` in node; there are no rendered
    component tests).
